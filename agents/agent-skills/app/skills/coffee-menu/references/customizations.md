# Customizations

- Skim

## Paid add-ons

| Add-on | Price |
|---|---|
| Extra espresso shot | $0.75 |

## Rules

- Maximum 2 syrup pumps on 12 oz; 3 on 16 oz.
- Extra hot or light ice: no charge.
- Do not combine automatic discounts unless a promotion explicitly allows stacking.
