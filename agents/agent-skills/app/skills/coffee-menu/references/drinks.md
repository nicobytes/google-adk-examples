# Drinks catalog

Available at **both** Downtown and Marina unless noted.

## Espresso-based

- **Espresso** — single or double shot
- **Americano** — espresso + hot water
- **Latte** — espresso + steamed milk
- **Cappuccino** — espresso + steamed milk + foam (equal parts)
- **Flat white** — double ristretto + microfoam

## Brewed and cold

- **Drip coffee** — rotating single-origin
- **Cold brew** — steeped 18 hours
- **Iced latte** — espresso + cold milk over ice

## Non-coffee

- **Hot chocolate** — Valrhona cocoa
- **Chai latte** — spiced concentrate + steamed milk

Not sold: blended frappes, energy drinks.
