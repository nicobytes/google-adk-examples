# Escalate to the team

## Instructions

Use this skill when:

1. **Catering or events** for 20 or more people.
2. **Refunds or charge disputes.**
3. **Complaints** about service or product quality that need follow-up.
4. **Menu items or prices** not found in any loaded reference.
5. **Severe allergy** accommodation beyond standard allergen notes.
6. **Holiday or emergency** hour changes not listed in `locations`.

What to do:

- Acknowledge the request with empathy.
- Do not promise refunds, discounts, or exceptions you cannot verify.
- Offer that a team member will follow up via email or phone.
- Repeat any useful details the customer already shared (location, date, order).
