# Coffee FAQs

## Do you offer decaf?

Yes. Any espresso drink can be made decaf at no extra charge.

## Is oat milk extra?

No. Oat, almond, and skim are free swaps.

## How much caffeine in cold brew?

A 16 oz cold brew has roughly 200 mg caffeine (estimate; not medical advice).

## Can I bring my own cup?

Yes. We discount $0.25 when you bring a clean reusable cup.

## Do you sell beans?

Yes. 12 oz bags of the current single-origin rotate weekly; ask in store for price.
