# Food FAQs

## When are pastries delivered?

Classic croissants and muffins arrive by 7:00 AM at both stores. Almond croissants
arrive at Marina by 8:00 AM only.

## Can I order almond croissant at Downtown?

No. Almond croissant is **Marina only** (see `food-menu` / `pastries.md`).

## Do you reheat pastries?

We can warm a croissant on request; muffins are served at room temperature.

## Do you serve lunch?

No full lunch menu. Sourdough toast (Downtown only) and pastries are available
until sold out.
