# Allergens

## Almond croissant (Marina only)

Contains: **wheat, milk, eggs, almonds**.

## Classic butter croissant

Contains: **wheat, milk, eggs**.

## Blueberry muffin

Contains: **wheat, milk, eggs**. May contain traces of nuts.

## General

- Harbor Roast is not a nut-free facility.
- We do not have certified gluten-free baked goods.
- For severe allergies, recommend speaking with a barista in store or use `escalate`.
